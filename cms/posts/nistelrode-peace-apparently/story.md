> Learn how to think. Learn how to write. Writing is the crystallization of thinking and clear thinking is how you make good decisions when life gives you a set of less than ideal options.
>
> — Some wise guy on the internet

I came across this quote on social media a few months ago. I’d happily attribute the author if I knew who they were, but whoever put those words together has been lost somewhere in my endless scrolling on Twitter (_yes, I still call it Twitter_), Reddit, Instagram or whichever app happened to have my attention that day.
Shoutout to “_Doomscrolling_” btw.

It was one of those posts that lingered in my head. Writing hasn’t occupied much of my time these past few years. Other pursuits did, and rightly so. Or maybe I havent been doing a lot of thinking lately and that would explain some of the decisions I've made over the past few years.

In fairness, I'm rather fond of most of them except for a few.

Now, with a little more time on my hands (for the next few months, at least) and just enough knowledge to build this little corner of the internet, returning to it felt less like starting something new and more like picking up a conversation I’d left unfinished.

I put this photo on my Instagram story. J's response was, verbatim,

> this is what peace looks like.

I hadn’t thought of it that way but I would be genuinely dishonest if I said I didn’t agree with her after giving it careful thought.

Where life goes from here is anybody's guess but that seems like tomorrow’s problem

Today, we feast!
